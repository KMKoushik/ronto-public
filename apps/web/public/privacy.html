Ronto

Privacy

Effective September 5, 2026.

About Ronto

Ronto is a private assistant for invited family members. It is not offered to the general public.

Google data Ronto accesses

After a family member gives permission, Ronto may access:

  • Gmail messages, drafts, labels, and supported mail settings.
  • Google Calendar calendars, events, settings, and access-control information.
  • OAuth connection details needed to keep the account connected.

How Google data is used

Ronto uses Google data only to answer a family member’s request or perform an action that the family member requested. Relevant content may be sent to the configured AI inference provider solely to complete that request. Ronto does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are encrypted and stored on Ronto’s private server. Results requested by a family member may appear in that family’s Ronto conversation history or memory. Google data is shared only with infrastructure and AI service providers needed to operate Ronto and fulfill the family member’s request.

Your control

Family members can disconnect a Google account from Ronto and can revoke Ronto’s access at any time from their Google Account permissions. Disconnecting removes the stored connector credentials from Ronto.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent through the contact method published by the operator of your Ronto deployment.
